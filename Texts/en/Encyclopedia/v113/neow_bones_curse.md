# The Final Curse from Neow's Bones

When filtering for the final Curse, specify {{relic:WHETSTONE}} or {{relic:WAR_PAINT}} in the capsule conditions if you plan to take it. Each relic advances the stream Bones uses for its Curse by one draw. If you take one you left out of the conditions, the Curse you get may differ from the prediction.

An extra upgradeable card can change which cards the relic upgrades. The shuffle still takes one draw from your `NonCombatGeneration` stream, so the Curse draw stays at the same position. See [[neow-offer]] for the order of the two Bones relics.
