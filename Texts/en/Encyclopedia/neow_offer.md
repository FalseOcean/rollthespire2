# {{relic:NEOWS_BONES}} Pickup Order

Bones offers two relics. Knowing their identities is sometimes not enough to determine the results inside them. Their effects resolve as you take them, and taking one first can affect what the other gives you. For example, taking {{relic:SILKEN_TRESS}} before {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} applies Glam to Kaleidoscope's first card group. In the opposite order, Silken Tress is not yet present when Kaleidoscope generates its rewards.

Often, you care about getting the result you want, without caring which relic you take first. I want to preserve that freedom, so when the order is unrestricted, RT2 calculates both pickup orders and checks their results. This gives the same seed another opportunity to satisfy your requirements and may help you find a usable opening sooner.

“Any order” means at least one pickup order satisfies all your conditions together. Follow the order shown in the result when you play. If you specifically want to take one relic first, you can set that order directly.

What matters here is the **pickup order**. You usually do not need to require Bones to generate or display one relic first, because you can choose which one to take first yourself. See [[neow-bones-curse]] for how your subsequent choices affect the final curse, and [[neow]] for the broader aim of Neow filtering.
