# Shops: What Will Each Visit Sell?

Use the Shop filter to find Colorless cards or shop-exclusive relics in the first few shops. The shop offers one Uncommon and one Rare Colorless card and draws its exclusive relic from a separate bag. Character cards also use `Shops`, but earlier combat card rewards change the rarity adjustment and can change which cards are for sale.

For the ordinary relics a shop can sell, start at the back of the personal relic queues in Seed Information. The shop uses `Shops` to roll rarity for those two slots, then takes an eligible relic from the back of the matching bag. If you claim your target from a chest first, you already have what you came for, and the shop will move on to the next eligible relic. RT2 does not yet filter or list the ordinary relics on the shelves.

## Count Shops You Actually Enter

RT2 filters the initial stock of the first **five normal shops**. Shop-exclusive relics, Uncommon colorless cards, and Rare colorless cards each have their own range. You can specify a visit or look for a target anywhere within the range. For example, look for a Shop-exclusive relic in the first three shops and a particular Rare colorless card in shop two.

Count **the normal shops you actually enter**. Walking past a shop leaves the count alone. Once you find a target in shop two, you still need a route that lets you visit two shops. In multiplayer, each player's inventory follows their own state.

Each shop starts where the previous shop left Shops **after generating its full inventory**. Fake Merchant prices and Courier restocks can move the stream a few steps farther. See [[shop-stability]] and, for how shops take relics from bags, [[relic-shop-consumption]].
