# Orobas: Branches, {{relic:SEA_GLASS}}, and Conditional Options

Looking for the Fool's Tangled Glass? Orobas has to offer {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} and draw the Fool as its target. Each of his three slots works a little differently. The odds below are for Standard mode. See [[reading-guide]] for the play settings.

## First Slot: Pick a Special Candidate, Then Draw One of Three

{{relic:ELECTRIC_SHRYMP}} and {{relic:GLASS_EYE}} are always in the pool. The third candidate is {{relic:PRISMATIC_GEM}} one third of the time and {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} two thirds of the time. Orobas then draws one of the three with equal odds.

| Relic | Offer probability |
| --- | --- |
| {{relic:ELECTRIC_SHRYMP}} | 1/3, about 33.33% |
| {{relic:GLASS_EYE}} | 1/3, about 33.33% |
| {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} | 2/9, about 22.22% |
| {{relic:PRISMATIC_GEM}} | 1/9, about 11.11% |

All four can show up. {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} comes up twice as often as {{relic:PRISMATIC_GEM}}.

### {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} Also Draws a Character

The game first draws from your other unlocked characters. If {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} takes the first slot, it uses that character's card pool. With all six characters unlocked, excluding your current character leaves five possible targets. The chance of **Sea Glass with one specific target** is **2/9 × 1/5 = 2/45, about 4.44%**. If you're playing another character and have unlocked the Fool, choose the Fool as your Sea Glass target in RT2 to look for Tangled Glass. With fewer unlocks, use the number of characters actually in the draw.

## Second Slot: One of Four

{{relic:ALCHEMICAL_COFFER}}, {{relic:DRIFTWOOD}}, {{relic:RADIANT_PEARL}}, and {{relic:SAND_CASTLE}} each have a **25%** chance.

## Third Slot: Do You Still Have the Starter Relic or Card?

{{relic:TOUCH_OF_OROBAS}} requires you to still own one of these starting relics.

| Starting relic | Improved relic |
| --- | --- |
| {{relic:BURNING_BLOOD}} | {{relic:BLACK_BLOOD}} |
| {{relic:RING_OF_THE_SNAKE}} | {{relic:RING_OF_THE_DRAKE}} |
| {{relic:DIVINE_RIGHT}} | {{relic:DIVINE_DESTINY}} |
| {{relic:BOUND_PHYLACTERY}} | {{relic:PHYLACTERY_UNBOUND}} |
| {{relic:CRACKED_CORE}} | {{relic:INFUSED_CORE}} |
| {{relic:SONG_BOW}} | {{relic:STORYTELLER}} |

{{relic:ARCHAIC_TOOTH}} checks whether the corresponding starting card is still in your deck.

| Starting card | Replacement |
| --- | --- |
| {{card:BASH}} | {{card:BREAK}} |
| {{card:NEUTRALIZE}} | {{card:SUPPRESS}} |
| {{card:UNLEASH}} | {{card:PROTECTOR}} |
| {{card:FALLING_STAR}} | {{card:METEOR_SHOWER}} |
| {{card:DUALCAST}} | {{card:QUADCAST}} |
| {{card:FOOLISH_SHOT}} | {{card:DIVINE_SHOT}} |

With both targets present, each relic has a **50%** chance. With one target present, its option is guaranteed. With neither, the third slot is locked. A Fool who lost Song Bow and kept Foolish Shot will see Archaic Tooth, which can turn it into Divine Shot.

When filtering the third slot, use the relics and deck you expect to have when you reach Orobas. See [[ancient]] for eligibility and [[why-predictable]] for how the draws follow the seed.
