# Neow: Offers and Opening Results

Neow filters answer two questions: **Which relics are offered, and what happens when you take them?** Some relics need only an identity check. Others generate cards, Capsule relics, potions, or transformations.

## Inputs for Prediction

There is very little history before Neow. With the seed, character, unlock state, and other required inputs, RT2 can replay the offer and the chosen relic's random effects. Different effects use different random streams. Bones pickup order, card picks, and transformation targets can also be premises for later results. See [[why-predictable]] for the shared principle and [[reading-guide]] for the encyclopedia's version and environment.

## Rare Openings Can Still Be Cheap to Check

Rarity and computation per seed are separate quantities. Many Neow conditions need only a short replay but reject almost every seed. Independently recoverable results can be checked early: transformations, for example, can be checked before whether Neow offers their source relic. Every condition must still hold, and gameplay pickup order is unchanged.
