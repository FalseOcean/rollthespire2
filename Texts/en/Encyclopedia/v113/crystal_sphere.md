# Crystal Sphere Seed Search and In-Run Guidance

You can work out which cards Crystal Sphere can offer before starting a run. Once you know the character, unlocks, and relevant relics on entry, the seed determines the board and reachable card rewards. Reveal order affects the rewards, so RT2 checks possible sequences of flips when it verifies candidate seeds.

## Find a seed for the rewards you want

“Uncover Future” costs gold and gives you 3 flips. “Payment Plan” gives you 6 flips and adds Debt. Both options start with the same board layout. Pick an option in seed search, then select one Uncommon card, one Rare card, or both. For a relic reward, choose 6 flips. The filter can check the rarity rolled. The relic itself comes from the bag you enter with.

Select the relics you expect to hold on entry. {{relic:DRIFTWOOD}} lets you reroll card rewards, {{relic:PRISMATIC_GEM}} adds cards from other unlocked characters, and {{relic:DINGY_RUG}} adds Colorless cards. Choose at most one of Driftwood and Prismatic Gem. Dingy Rug can go with either or on its own.

During the run you'll need to reach Crystal Sphere with the chosen relics. See [[event-appearance|Event appearance]] for getting to the event.

## Follow the board during a run

Enable “In-run tools button” in Settings and click the Bones icon at the top right during the event. The assistant reads your own board in solo or multiplayer. You can compare the two branches before starting or continue from the board and flips you have left. After choosing targets and starting guidance, select the branch in the game, switch tools and click the marked cells in order, then collect your rewards. Turn on “Avoid board curses” if you want the guide to go around the board's curse.

You can select a reward as soon as it turns up. The assistant checks subsequent candidates against the targets you've chosen, and you can click a gray candidate to check it individually. Pause and resume at any time. You can keep looking for a target the assistant hasn't found yet. Closing and reopening on the same board keeps your selection and plans. With no rewards selected, the assistant looks for the most gold it can find. Once you select rewards, it keeps those targets while optimizing gold. The maximum is marked only when proven. If you pause, it keeps the best plan found so far.

Check whether a card target is the normal or upgraded version. The assistant uses the rewards generated from the state when you opened it, including eggs you already hold and natural upgrades. Turn on “Exact enchantments” to distinguish unenchanted cards from specific enchantments and amounts. Changing that option clears the targets and starts a new search. Eggs, {{relic:WHETSTONE}} or {{relic:WAR_PAINT}} obtained here still work normally. Card targets refer to the versions generated before you took those relics.

If you have Driftwood, the assistant enables rerolls by default. After flipping, reroll the indicated rewards and use Skip after each to return to the reward list. Collect the planned rewards after finishing the rerolls. With {{relic:PAELS_WING}}, the assistant can also use your current sacrifice count to find sacrifice relics you can take alongside your other goals. Each sacrifice uses up a whole card reward, and two sacrifices yield a relic. Follow the marked steps for taking and sacrificing rewards. Driftwood rerolls and Pael's Wing sacrifices are currently solved separately.

Entering the planned branch from the preview keeps the plan. If the actual flips, card rewards or relevant random state diverge, reopen the assistant to read the scene again. The guidance mask is a separate option and starts off. Your settings for board curses, Driftwood rerolls, exact enchantments and the mask carry over to later events. See [[reading-guide|Reading and scope guide]] for environment details.
