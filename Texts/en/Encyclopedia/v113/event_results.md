# Which Event Outcomes Can RT2 Filter?

Sometimes what you want from an event is a particular reward. Many events have their own random stream, so the seed and player slot are enough to work out which doll you could get in Doll Room. For where an event sits in the queue and what makes it eligible to appear, see [[event-appearance|Event appearance]].

| Event | What you can look for |
| --- | --- |
| Colorful Philosophers | Whether the first set of colors includes a particular character |
| Fake Merchant | A particular fake relic anywhere in the six-item inventory |
| Trash Heap | Which card or relic you get from the corresponding option |
| Trial | The case you draw after accepting the trial. The entrant number is cosmetic. |
| Tinker Time | The offered card types and which extra effects are available after picking a type |
| {{event:DOLL_ROOM}} | Which doll you get at random for no HP loss, or whether it appears among the two choices for 5 HP |
| {{event:MUSEUM_OF_TORTURE}} | Which of three relics is on display. Taking it also gives you a random Curse. |
| {{event:THERMAE_NONUPEIPE}} | Which of nine relics you get for drinking the bath water |
| {{event:CRYSTAL_SPHERE}} | Reachable card rewards and the rarity rolled for a relic reward |

For the pools and odds behind Colorful Philosophers, Fake Merchant and Trash Heap, see [[event-stable-results|Stable event outcomes]]. [[crystal-sphere|Crystal Sphere]] covers its targets and board rules.

An event like Relic Trader first rolls Common, Uncommon or Rare, then draws from the relic bag. The filter checks **the tier rolled**. The item itself depends on what's left in the bag. These filters cover Battleworn Dummy, Luminous Choir, Ranwid the Elder, Relic Trader, Round Tea Party, This or That, Unrest Site, Trial and Waterfall of Obvious Secrets. Play the corresponding branch, such as selecting Setting 3 and winning within the time limit at Battleworn Dummy. For a branch with multiple rolls, you can pick a specific roll or require the tier on any or all of them. Relic Trader shows three offers and lets you complete one trade.

For transformed cards, you can check one result each from Symbiote, Aroma of Chaos and Whispering Hollow, or two each from the relevant options in Morphic Grove and Trial. RT2 assumes you choose legal starter cards at the event, including character-specific starters from the same transformation pool. See [[event-transform-results|Event transformations]] for the choices and targets, or [[transform|Combined transformations]] to count cards from several sources together.
