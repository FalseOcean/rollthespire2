# Stable Event Outcomes: Colorful Philosophers, Fake Merchant and Trash Heap

If you're looking for a particular card in Trash Heap, you can check a seed without playing through the earlier fights or keeping count of your shop visits. These filters answer what you'll see once you enter the event. See [[event-appearance|Event appearance]] for when you can encounter it.

## The Colorful Philosophers' colors

The game leaves out your own character's pool and offers up to three colors from the unlocked pools. With all six pools unlocked, including the Fool's, it picks three of the other five. Any one of those colors has a **3/5 (60%)** chance to appear. With four other pools eligible the chance is **3/4 (75%)**, and when there are three or fewer, you see them all.

Changing character, unlocks or player slot can change the offers even on the same seed. Once you pick a color, the game generates a Common, an Uncommon and a Rare card reward from that pool. If you want access to a particular character's cards, look for their color first.

## The Fake Merchant's six items

Fake Merchant shuffles a fixed set of **9 fake relics** and sells the first **6**. Any particular relic has a **6/9 (2/3)** chance to appear, anywhere in the inventory.

Fake Merchant appears in single-player runs. The event draws its own inventory, so earlier shop visits leave those six identities alone. The prices use your `Shops` stream, though, so a visit can still change the random sequence for later shops even if you buy nothing.

## A card or a relic from Trash Heap?

The card option picks one of **10 fixed cards**, with a **10%** chance for each. The relic option picks one of **5 fixed relics**, with a **20%** chance for each. The ten cards are the same across characters, and the relic comes straight from that set of five.

Both options start from the same event random state. You can compare what each would yield and then take the one you want when you play.

Colorful Philosophers and Trash Heap start their streams from your player slot, so a teammate might see a different result on the same seed. See [[multiplayer-shared|Shared party conditions]] for how that works and [[event-results|Event outcomes]] for more filters.
