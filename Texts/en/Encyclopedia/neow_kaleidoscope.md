# What Is {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} Generation Order Useful For?

The reason is simple: in the default environment, distinguishing {{relic-icon:KALEIDOSCOPE}}'s two groups by generation order is mainly there to help you find an opening that reliably gives a specified Rare card from another character the Glam enchantment.

To search for this, specify Kaleidoscope's generation order and require **the target card in the first group**, then require {{relic-icon:SILKEN_TRESS}} as the other relic inside {{relic-icon:NEOWS_BONES}}. When playing, take Silken Tress first, then Kaleidoscope. Tress enchants Kaleidoscope's first reward group with Glam; choose the target from that group to obtain the enchanted card. Taking Kaleidoscope before Tress will not produce this effect.

“First group” means the group generated first, rather than the group you open or choose from first. Kaleidoscope generates both groups before displaying the rewards, so changing your click order will not move Glam to the other group. See [[neow-offer]] for relic pickup order, and [[neow-bones-curse]] for why Kaleidoscope card choices can also affect the Bones curse.
