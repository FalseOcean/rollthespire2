# Combat Rewards: What Does “Consecutive” Mean?

I want players to be able to search for “this card in combat one,” “this card within the first three combats,” or “this potion in combat two.” RT2 can look up card and potion rewards from the first **1–6 normal combats**. To pin down which combat gives you a reward, we need to know what happened between fights.

Consecutive here mostly means **continuity of the Rewards stream**. Start with the chosen Neow opening, then generate normal-combat rewards in order, without extra Rewards draws or changes to the reward rules along the way. Start with {{relic-icon:PRAYER_WHEEL}} and there's an extra card reward to generate after each normal fight. Both sequences are possible to calculate. See [[combat-neow]] for the opening choices.

Combat → Rest Site → Combat works if the rest-site action leaves the reward state alone. So does stepping into a normal shop just to look. The stock uses Shops. Buy a relic that changes combat rewards, though, and the later fights have to account for that new relic.

## What Can Change the Next Combat's Rewards?

Slip in an Elite fight and its rewards use Rewards too. For a question mark, it depends what you find and what you choose. Many events have their own stream. Take an option that generates an ordinary card or potion reward and you may use some of Rewards ahead of the next fight.

Some changes happen inside the fight, where the map gives you no clue.

- {{card-icon:THE_HUNT}} adds a three-card reward when you trigger Fatal. Generating that reward uses random draws and updates the card rarity adjustment.
- {{card-icon:ROYALTIES}} adds a separate post-combat gold reward. The amount is fixed, but generating that reward still uses Rewards.
- Killing a thief to recover stolen gold also adds a separate gold reward, which can advance Rewards in the same way.

To count the extra cards from The Hunt, you'd need to draw it, play it, and get the kill. That's harder to plan than carrying Prayer Wheel from the start.

That's why I started with a sequence of normal combats that's easy to follow in play. Set up the opening and look for the card or potion you want, and you can already answer a lot of questions about what shows up in the first few fights. For reward relics, Rare rates, and potion drops, see [[combat-rarity-relics]].
