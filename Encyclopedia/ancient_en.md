# Ancients: Encounters and Offers

Ancient filters can require an identity in a specified Act, particular offered relics, and several relics **together in the same offer**. Act 1 is Neow; see [[neow]] for its mechanics, including {{relic:NEOWS_BONES}} and {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}}. Act 2 and 3 identities belong to world generation, while offers follow each Ancient's own rules.

## Eligibility Determines the Pool First

Some relics require particular deck or ownership facts to be eligible. RT2 uses those premises to build the actual pool before generating options. **Marking a relic unavailable removes it from the pool; it differs from asking not to draw it** and can change other relics' probabilities.

Premises describe the state upon reaching the Ancient, rather than the starting deck. Orobas, for example, requires retained starting relics or cards; some Pael options depend on enchantability, removability, and Event Pet ownership. See [[why-predictable]] for the shared seed and random-stream principle and [[reading-guide]] for the default environment.

## One Target or Several Together

One target needs to appear in the offer. Advanced mode's co-offer condition requires every target in the same offer. Targets from a position that selects only one relic may be mutually exclusive. RT2 checks the actual offer structure instead of combining their individual possibilities.

Offer identity, special targets, and effects after pickup are also distinct. Filtering an offered relic does not imply support for every random effect after obtaining it.
