# Reading Guide

I want as many of your requirements for a seed as possible to become things you can search for. A particular Neow relic or a card in a shop is easy to ask for. Other results depend on what you actually take and which options you choose. That's where a question I care about comes up: what kind of seed are we actually looking for?

If you want a pair of relics from Bones to give you a particular result, you can let the seed finder try both pickup orders. One order that works gives you an opening you can follow. Asking both orders to work is a different question. Some conditions that apply to every route are straightforward to compute, though. You can read the fewest fights you'll have to take directly from the map. See [[neow-offer|Bones pickup order]] and [[map-routes|Map routes]].

Putting every action into the query would let us calculate more, but searching for one seed could turn into filling out a questionnaire. There are decisions I make only after seeing the map or the rewards. I'd rather use the seed finder for useful information whose premises are easy to describe. Once the choice is in front of you, see what prediction can tell you about it.

If you already have a seed, Seed Information shows Neow, the map, and other opening results. During a run, operation prediction previews the choice in front of you, and the [[crystal-sphere|Crystal Sphere assistant]] helps with the board and reward plans.

The [[default-environment|Default environment]] page links to each kind of filter. See [[multiplayer|Multiplayer]], [[mods|Mods]], or the [[faq|FAQ]] if you're bringing teammates, playing with mods, or trying to understand a result. For how the game makes these results predictable, see [[why-predictable|Prediction principles]].
