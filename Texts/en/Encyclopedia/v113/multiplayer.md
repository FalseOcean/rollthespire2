# Multiplayer: One Seed and the Party's Choices

Since 0.113.0, many Neow effects have used each player's own random stream. If two players choose Bones, either can claim theirs first without advancing the other player's stream. Pay attention to the order you join the lobby. Players fill seats as they join, and personal results depend on the seat.

Set shared conditions such as maps and bosses once, then fill in personal openings and rewards by seat. RT2 can read unlocks from the current online lobby. Set the characters and the run's Ascension yourself, and read the lobby again if the players or seats change. See [[multiplayer-shared|Shared party conditions]].
