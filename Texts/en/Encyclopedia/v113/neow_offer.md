# {{relic:NEOWS_BONES}} Pickup Order

Neow's Bones gives you two relics, and you choose which to claim first. Take {{relic:SILKEN_TRESS}} before {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} and the Tress affects the first group of cards Kaleidoscope generates. Take them the other way around and both groups are generated before you have the Tress.

Often you just want the outcome and don't mind which relic comes first. Leave the order unrestricted and RT2 checks both ways of claiming them. A seed qualifies if either order meets all your conditions at once. Follow the order shown in the result when you play. If you already know which one you want first, you can specify it.

If you and a teammate both take Bones, you can each claim yours whenever you're ready. The order in the Tress and Kaleidoscope example is the order of your own two relics.

Bones draws the curse after both relic effects are resolved. If you plan to take Whetstone or War Paint from a capsule, include it when filtering for [[neow-bones-curse|the final Curse]].
