# Neow Overview

Neow is an Ancient you must encounter in Act 1. I want RT2's filtering of this opening to cover more than which three options Neow offers. I also want to filter the specific contents of each option and predict the possible results of Neow as accurately as I can.

That brings the rewards inside an option, the order in which you take relics, and the card choices that follow into the discussion. These articles explain how to express those requirements and why I currently make the corresponding tradeoffs:

- [[neow-transform]]: New Leaf transformation targets and the starting-card convention.
