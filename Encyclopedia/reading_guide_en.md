# Reading and Scope Guide

Ordinary game mechanics here use **Slay the Spire 2 Beta 0.111.0, vanilla, full unlocks and single-player** as the explanatory baseline. This does not limit RT2's supported environments.

Actual predictions depend on the query and run's version, character, slot, ascension, unlocks and content pools. Relevant articles describe substantial multiplayer or mod differences. An unlisted difference is not proof that every environment was verified; an unverified case does not mean the entire environment is unusable. See [[multiplayer|Multiplayer]] and [[mods|Mods]].

Distinguish the game's generation rules, the features RT2 currently offers, and the premises required to use a result. Necessary action instructions remain in the relevant mechanics article.

For seeds, hashes, filtering, prediction and the older versions' seed space, see [[seed-history|Seeds: What Are We Searching?]].

For symptoms or quick premise checks, read [[faq|FAQ]]; follow its links to understand the mechanics. Start with [[why-predictable|Why prediction works]] for the principles, or [[crystal-sphere|Crystal Sphere]] for the current in-run feature.
