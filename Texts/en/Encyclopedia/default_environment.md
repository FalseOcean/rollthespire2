# Default Environment

The default environment is **vanilla, single-player, with all official Epochs unlocked**. Act and encounter discovery states are separate inputs; “all unlocks” here does not include them.

In this environment, you express your filtering requirements and follow the action conventions RT2 explains in advance; RT2 promises that confirmed results will satisfy those requirements. The explanations under each filtering page describe why its conventions are needed, which choices affect the results, and what the filtering confirms.

Confirmation comes from RT2's independent model checks. Exact in the program does not mean running a complete run in the real game. Normal checks may also deliver unconfirmed candidates because of implementation gaps or other reasons, including in the default environment. An unknown outcome is neither a proven mismatch nor confirmation of a match. You can save unconfirmed candidates as favorites with their unconfirmed status retained.
