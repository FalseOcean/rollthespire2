# How Shops Consume Relic Queues

A normal shop starts with three relic slots. The first two use Shops to roll Common, Uncommon, or Rare. The third requests Shop rarity. All three take relics from the **back** of the player's matching bags.

## Two Ends of the Same Bag

RT2 displays Common, Uncommon, and Rare queues from the front. In a bag ordered A → B → C → D → E, a reward drawing from the front reaches A first. If a shop rolls that rarity, it starts looking at E. Both ends draw from the same personal bag. Chests use a separate shared bag, but claiming a chest relic also removes it from your personal bag. When a relic goes on a shop shelf first, it comes out of the shared bag too.

The merchant skips relics that can't be sold. {{relic:LUCKY_FYSH}}, {{relic:AMETHYST_AUBERGINE}}, {{relic:BOWLER_HAT}}, {{relic:OLD_COIN}}, and {{relic:THE_COURIER}} stay off the shelf even when they're at the back of a bag. The first four affect gold gains, and the Courier changes discounts and restocking.

RT2's Shop-exclusive queue already follows the merchant's draw order and skips unsellable relics. The first entry is the first one a normal merchant can take for the exclusive slot.

## Entering the Shop and Buying the Relic

By the time a relic is on the shelf, it has already left the bag. Walk out without buying it and the next reward using that bag still won't find it there. The first two slots roll for rarity, then take the relic according to bag order. Buying it is what grants the relic and triggers its on-obtain effects.

With {{relic:THE_COURIER}}, buying a relic restocks the slot. Shops rolls Common, Uncommon, or Rare, and the merchant takes the replacement from the back of that bag. Those are the three rarities it rolls even when you bought the Shop-exclusive relic in slot three. See [[shop-stability]] for what restocking means for later stock.
