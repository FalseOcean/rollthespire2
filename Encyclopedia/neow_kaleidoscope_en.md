# {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}}: Opening Cards from Other Characters

## What You Want

{{relic-icon:KALEIDOSCOPE}} lets you obtain **other characters' cards** at the beginning of a run.

It generates two groups of cards. Each group contains one card from each of three other characters' pools. This enables opening goals that are normally difficult to achieve:

> Start as my current character with a particular card from another character.

You can also require multiple specified cards to appear.

## Why It Can Be Predicted

{{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} happens at Neow, with almost no prior history to reconstruct.

For each group, the game uses **Niche RNG** to determine which other character pools to use, then **Rewards RNG** to generate a card from each pool.

Each card first receives a random rarity, followed by a specific identity from that character's pool at that rarity.

These processes can be replayed directly from the seed, allowing RT2 to check the cards generated by {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}}.

## Why Specified Rare Cards Can Be Extremely Unlikely

{{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} is inexpensive to calculate, but can produce extraordinarily rare conditions.

A specified Rare card requires several conditions in succession:

> Select the corresponding character pool
> → Roll Rare for this card
> → Draw the specified card from that character's Rare pool

The game has multiple characters, each with a complete card pool.

Rare generation itself has a low probability. Under the current rules, ordinary card generation has a Rare rate of **3% at A0–A6**, dropping to **1.49% at A7+**.

Requiring **two specified Rare cards from {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}}** combines both character-pool, rarity, and identity requirements. The probability can easily shrink by several orders of magnitude.

This illustrates its distinctive nature:

**The opening can be exceptionally rare, while checking a seed still takes only a short random replay.**

## What RT2 Can Do Today

RT2 can filter for specified cards from {{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} and distinguish the order of its two groups when needed.

You can seek a flexible cross-character opening or a very rare combination such as two specified Rare cards.

{{relic:KALEIDOSCOPE}} demonstrates a recurring point in RT2:

> **How rare a condition is and how difficult it is to compute are separate questions.**
