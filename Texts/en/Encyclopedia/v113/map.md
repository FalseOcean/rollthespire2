# Maps: How Do You Describe a Good Map?

“This is a good map” is easy to say and hard to turn into a filter. The player hunting Elites, the one building up at Rest Sites, and the one trying their luck at question marks might head in three different directions on the same map.

I want the map filters to let you describe the route you want and the limits you'll accept. A single score would hide what makes a map worth playing to you. For a route with lots of Elites, set a count target for one Act or the full run. To cap Rest Sites on every route, set a map property. For a single path that takes you to a Shop, then an Elite, then a Rest Site, choose **Advanced drawing** in the map conditions for that Act. See [[map-routes]] for how to express each of these.

Search uses the standard map for your settings. Check Act variants such as {{act:OVERGROWTH}} and {{act:UNDERDOCKS}} with the Ascension and player count you plan to play. Each Act generates its standard map from its own seed-derived random stream, so your route through one Act leaves the next Act's standard map alone. Map-changing effects take effect as you acquire and use them.

Once you find a seed, open its map in Seed Information to browse, draw, and confirm routes. With the current and earlier Acts' routes confirmed, you can see what room types the ordinary question marks will turn into. If one opens into an event, the event queue and your eligibility on arrival determine which one you meet. See [[event-appearance]].

This is how I've organized the map preferences so far. If you read maps differently, I'd like to know what conditions would better describe the route you want to play.
