# Relics in Treasure Rooms

> Mechanic reference: Slay the Spire 2 Beta 0.111.0

Normal chests draw from **shared relic bags**, separate from the player's personal Common, Uncommon, and Rare bags described in [[relics]]. Single-player also has these separate shared bags.

A chest first uses its chest RNG to roll Common, Uncommon, or Rare, then takes an identity from the front of the matching shared bag. The result depends on both rarity and earlier shared-bag consumption. The first personal Rare relic does not establish the first Rare chest's contents.

## Filtering the first two chests

Switch to chest filtering on the Relics page to specify targets for the first chest, the second, or both. Chests are counted in **actual encounter order**, including question marks that become treasure rooms, rather than by the fixed chest in each Act. Ordered targets correspond to positions within the selected range; unordered targets only need to occur within that range. Empty slots add no requirement.

The purpose remains to help you obtain the relics you want. A target already obtained during the same matching Neow opening can also satisfy this acquisition requirement; the result identifies its actual opening source, rather than promising another copy from a chest. If {{relic:SILVER_CRUCIBLE}} suppresses a chest's relic, that chest still occupies its encounter position. Later rewards do not move forward to replace it.

This filter covers normal, non-tutorial single-player runs, using eligibility before floor 41 and accounting for known opening acquisitions. Other sources consuming shared bags or changing eligibility along the way can still change actual chest results. Plan around those effects; this filter does not simulate the entire playthrough for you.

## The nine-entry initial reference

The nine entries in Seed Information are a sequence of reference draws from the initial bags with eligibility before floor 41, ignoring opening and later acquisitions. They help you inspect initial opportunities, but do not mean nine-chest filtering is available or replace the first-two-chest result that accounts for your opening.
