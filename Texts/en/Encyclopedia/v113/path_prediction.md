# Route Forecast: Rewards and Choices Along Your Line

To pick a route for a seed, open its map in **Seed Information**. Hold the right mouse button to draw, release to preview, and click **Select route** to confirm it. You can also browse routes with the most Elites or Rest Sites. Once the current and earlier Acts' routes are selected, you can see which room types the ordinary question marks will turn into. The specific event still depends on the queue and your eligibility when you get there. See [[event-appearance|Event appearance]].

Once you're in a room, hover over a supported choice to see its **operation preview**. At Orobas, for example, hover over {{relic:SEA_GLASS}} to see which cards you could take. This uses your actual state on arrival. You can choose which previews appear under **Operation predictions** in settings.

Drawing on the in-run map starts from your current Act in solo play. Looking ahead to rewards also depends on the cards you take, what you buy, and which event options you choose. When a choice is open, later results that depend on it stay open too. Support for forecasting rewards along the full line is still pending.

To specify which rooms a route should visit while filtering seeds, see [[map-routes|Map routes]].
