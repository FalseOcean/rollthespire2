# Act 3 Ancients: Candidate Lists and Fixed Positions

If you want an Act 3 Ancient to offer two specific relics, look at which pools they come from. {{event:NONUPEIPE}} and {{event:TANX}} shuffle one pool and take three relics. Each slot of {{event:VAKUU}} draws one from its own pool. The odds below are for Standard mode. See [[reading-guide]] for the play settings.

## {{event:NONUPEIPE}}: Nine Base Candidates

- {{relic:BLESSED_ANTLER}}
- {{relic:BRILLIANT_SCARF}}
- {{relic:DELICATE_FROND}}
- {{relic:DIAMOND_DIADEM}}
- {{relic:FUR_COAT}}
- {{relic:GLITTER}}
- {{relic:JEWELRY_BOX}}
- {{relic:LOOMING_FRUIT}}
- {{relic:SIGNET_RING}}

The game shuffles the pool and offers the first three. Each relic has a **3/9 = 1/3 chance, about 33.33%**.

With at least **4 Swift-enchantable cards** in your deck, {{relic:BEAUTIFUL_BRACELET}} joins the pool. There are still three offers, so all ten candidates now have a **3/10 = 30%** chance. If you only want one of the original nine, qualifying for the Bracelet makes it a little harder to find.

## {{event:TANX}}: Shuffle and Take Three

The nine base candidates are listed below.

- {{relic:CLAWS}}
- {{relic:CROSSBOW}}
- {{relic:IRON_CLUB}}
- {{relic:MEAT_CLEAVER}}
- {{relic:SAI}}
- {{relic:SPIKED_GAUNTLETS}}
- {{relic:TANXS_WHISTLE}}
- {{relic:THROWING_AXE}}
- {{relic:WAR_HAMMER}}

Each has a **1/3** chance. With at least **3 Instinct-enchantable cards** in your deck, {{relic:TRI_BOOMERANG}} joins the pool and each candidate's chance becomes **3/10 = 30%**.

## {{event:VAKUU}}: Three Fixed Slots

| Slot | Candidates | Probability each |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | {{relic:BLOOD_SOAKED_ROSE}}, {{relic:WHISPERING_EARRING}}, {{relic:FIDDLE}} | 1/3 |
| Second | {{relic:PRESERVED_FOG}}, {{relic:SERE_TALON}}, {{relic:DISTINGUISHED_CAPE}} | 1/3 |
| Third | {{relic:CHOICES_PARADOX}}, {{relic:MUSIC_BOX}}, {{relic:LORDS_PARASOL}}, {{relic:JEWELED_MASK}} | 1/4 |

Vakuu draws directly from each pool in slot order. If you want relics offered together, pick them from different slots. Two relics in the same row compete for the same slot.

When filtering Nonupeipe and Tanx, count the cards that can actually take the enchantment when you reach the Ancient. See [[ancient]] for targets and eligibility and [[why-predictable]] for how the draws follow the seed.
